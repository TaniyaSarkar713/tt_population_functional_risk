# Closest-paper matrix for the revised formulation

Updated 2026-09-09. Ranking measures novelty threat, not paper quality. The classical diagonal benchmark moves Collier–Comminges–Tsybakov to first place. The 20 rows distinguish the statistical experiments and record full-text inspection depth. Drton (2009) remains in the bibliography as background on singular models.
