# Assumption audit and relaxation matrix

Necessary for one proof is distinguished from necessary for every procedure. Counterexamples are in counterexamples.md.

| Assumption | Needed? | Used where | Weakened? | Final version | Justification/counterexample |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1a Full observation | Essential to current lower bounds and S7 | Gaussian likelihood on all entries; subtensor scan | No completion/regression transfer | Retain full Z, or calibrated replicates | The diagonal marginal is not a sufficient experiment for full-data optimality; D1 cannot be transferred. |
| A1b Gaussian law | Not needed for threshold geometry/coverage | T2 direct alternative; T3–T6 and S7–S8 likelihood/tails | S3 envelope; S4 finite variance | Envelope for nonlinear upper bound; explicit Gaussian model for current lower bounds | C3 gives finite-variance failure of unchanged Gaussian calibration. |
| A1c Coordinate independence | Not needed for nonlinear union bound | Exact direct variance and isotropic likelihood | S3 dependent margins; S6 known covariance | Use marginal envelope plus actual direct variance | C4: perfectly correlated errors make direct variance p times too small. |
| A1d Replicate independence | Needed by current S4/S5 proofs | Block concentration; Gaussian mean/residual factorization | Within-replicate dependence allowed in S4 | Independent clean replicate vectors | Serial/martingale versions require a replacement block-tail argument; not proved. |
| A1e Known noise scale | Needed for stated original radius | All original calibrations | S5 estimates a conservative scale from independent residuals | Known scale OR retained n>=2 Gaussian replicates with error split | Cannot insert a same-data scale estimate without recalibration. |
| A1f Isotropic covariance | Not needed for envelope upper bound | Original affine and mixture laws | S6 positive-semidefinite experiment comparison | Known marginal upper variances; covariance floor for transferred lower bound | C8 makes target noiseless if no lower covariance condition is imposed. |
| A2a Known norm cap M | Defines the outer class and useful threshold budget | T1/T2 structural and nonlinear radii; lower alternatives | Known outer bound, or approximation class; no automatic estimation | Retain supplied M | C6: wrong M can select a deterministic interval with zero coverage. The unconstrained diagonal image is all of R^p. |
| A2b Positive supplied rank caps | Essential to current geometry and honesty class | L1; T1; h0; class inclusion in lower bounds | Smaller actual ranks allowed; S1 improves cap coefficient | Retain upper caps and fixed TT mode order | C6: underspecified cap can force zero coverage. Zero caps give trivial class and violate rank-one-inclusion lower bounds. |
| A2c Exact low rank | Not essential for upper bound | Membership in geometric class | S3 distance class with known eta | dist_F(A,Theta(M,caps))<=eta; radius plus eta | Proved residual Lipschitz bound gives eta, not 2eta; C12 shows constant one can be necessary. |
| A2d Minimum singular value | Absent and unnecessary here | No inverse perturbation anywhere | No strengthening needed | No signal floor | Canonical factorizations are exact algebra; they do not estimate weak factors. |
| A2e Incoherence/condition number | Absent | No leverage upper bound or condition-number denominator required | No strengthening needed | Allow coherent and ill-conditioned tensors | Coordinate mixtures deliberately include maximally coherent rank-one tensors. |
| A3a Modewise orthonormal loading | Sufficient; strict form needed for original constants | Bessel geometry, h0, direct variance, image lemma | S1 frame factor product(F_k)^(1/K) | Retain orthogonality in main statements; explicit frame variant only for diagonal budget | C5 repeated directions destroy the dimension-free bound. |
| A3b Fixed loading | Essential to unadjusted guarantees | Noise law and selection | Independent training sample permitted by conditioning | Loading fixed or independent of inference sample | C7 data-selected maximum coordinate has coverage (1-alpha)^p at zero. |
| A3c Weight normalization | Normalization only | \|\|L\|\|=1 and approximation Lipschitz coefficient | Rescale target/radius for any nonzero fixed w | \|\|w\|\|_2=1 for stated formulas | Zero loading gives exact zero; unnormalized direct and approximation radii scale by \|\|w\|\|_2. |
| A3d Equal loading and equal modes | Needed for stated p-rate and lower mixtures | T3–T8; D1 | General orthogonal loading allowed in T1/T2/S1/S3–S5 | Repeat equal-loading condition explicitly in T6 and T8 | Arbitrary weights do not have the same sparse-mixture target or rate. |
| A4a Uniform finite-sample honesty | Defining requirement | All lower-bound alternatives and every upper bound | None: changing it changes the question | inf_A coverage >=1-alpha | Pointwise regular-stratum confidence cannot establish this requirement. |
| A4b Interval-valued output | Needed by common-target length argument | L5: both targets imply length at least their distance | Convex hull diameter alternative possible but different loss | Measurable intervals; independent randomization allowed | Disconnected sets can contain two points with zero Lebesgue measure; interval assumption matters. |
| A4c Selection independent of centers | Sufficient, not universally necessary | Original min-radius choice | S3 common-event selection; S5 residual conditioning; S7 intersection with error split | Use exactly the stated selection/calibration rule | C7 choosing between individually valid intervals can under-cover. |
| A4d Confidence level alpha | Specific constants have restricted domains | T3 alpha<1/2; T4/T5/T6/D1 alpha<1/4 | S6 all fixed alpha<1/2 after recalibration | Explicit 0<alpha<1/4 in T5 | C1 gives a literal counterexample at alpha=.9; no issue at the original .05. |
| A5a Fixed K>=3 | Needed for original asymptotic separation | Positive beta=(K-2)/(2K); S7 summability | Finite-sample T1/T2 hold K>=2 | K fixed for rate constants; K3 and K>=4 distinguished | At K2 joint and one-cut geometry coincide; no claimed vanishing ratio. |
| A5b Fixed caps | Not needed for finite-sample coverage | Treats cap factors as asymptotic constants | S1 condition D(log p/p)^beta ->0 | Track D or J explicitly for growing caps | No uniform-in-rank constant is claimed for S7/S8 rate comparison. |
| A5c M=tau sqrt(p) | A comparison regime, not necessary for coverage | Displayed sharp/split rates | All original nonasymptotic radii retain M,tau,p | Keep explicit norm-noise scaling for rates | General ratio u=M/(tau sqrt(p)) changes phase boundaries; C11 records sufficient gain condition. |
| S7 Finite predetermined nets | Needed by present scan proof | Measurability, cardinality, Gaussian union bound | Countable measurable nets also possible with a new calibration | Fixed epsilon=1/(2K) nets; all subset sizes | No unmeasurable supremum or unverified entropy theorem; finite volume argument provided. |
| S7 gamma shrinking with p | Needed for current zero-point expected-length rate | Controls rejection-width contribution ell_* gamma | Other gamma possible if contribution controlled | gamma=alpha/(2p) in asymptotic corollary | High-probability width alone does not imply short expectation. |
| S4 Known variance bound and count c | Needed by robust construction | Chebyshev threshold and number of spoiled blocks | Stochastic Bernoulli count conversion proved | b>=8c, b>=32log(m/alpha), n>=2b | Sufficient and conservative; no optimal breakdown claim. |
| S5 Retained residuals and n>=2 | Needed by that scale estimator | chi-square law and conditional selection | No Z-only variance theorem claimed | nu=m(n-1); independent residual calibration | Gaussian mean is sufficient for A with known sigma, not for the joint unknown-variance experiment. |
