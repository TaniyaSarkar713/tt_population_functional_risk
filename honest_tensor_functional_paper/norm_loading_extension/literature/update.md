# Positioning of the norm and loading extension

The focused search on 2026-09-11 used combinations of tensor linear functionals, Gaussian means, norm budgets, minimax length, diagonal contrasts, and honest rank-bound inference. It did not identify a directly equivalent all-budget theorem in the inspected results. This search is not an exhaustive priority review.

The argument is grounded in the already downloaded [closest references](../../../references/README.md):

- [Collier, Comminges, and Tsybakov (2017)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.00665): Gaussian functional estimation on sparse and quasi-norm classes, including norm-dependent regimes. The new claim concerns a full tensor experiment and its ordered-entry constraints; the diagonal marginal benchmark remains classical.
- [Cai and Low (2004), adaptation theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0503662): expected-length lower bounds under outer-class honesty. Neither confidence-set projection nor common-target testing is proposed as a new general principle.
- [Cai and Low (2004), nonconvex functional estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0406427): nonlinear advantages on structured nonconvex classes are prior methodology.
- [Xu, Chen, and Han (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16223): regular-signal tensor functional inference permits broader loadings. Our new weighted results still assume a prescribed orthogonal product representation and do not dominate that regime.
- [Choo and d'Orsi (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.06308) and [Fu and Sohn (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30113): restricted computational frameworks. The new residual estimator supplies statistical existence only and does not solve the efficient-inference gap.

The concrete additions are the norm-sensitive ordered diameter, the norm-tuned use of the existing sparse harmonic tensor mixture, uniform MSE control through residual excess, and a weight-equalizing contraction of full-tensor hard alternatives. Their mathematical correctness and priority need independent review. No new external theorem was inserted as an unchecked proof dependency.
