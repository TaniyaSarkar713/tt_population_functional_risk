D. Theory changes
No central theorem rate or original assumption was silently changed. The status ledger contains 59 retained
PROVED results plus one new PROVED proposition, R1. These are complete internally checked supplied
proofs, not formal verification or external peer review.

intersection. It bounds unconditional squared risk by C_alpha min(h²,tau²), without pilot-success conditioning,
separation or candidate independence. At h￿tau it uses zero; otherwise it uses the intersection midpoint, with
clipped direct estimation for an empty/zero sentinel. The complete pointwise tau² r/p risk claim remains open.

replicates, known row corruption and independent Gaussian residual scale. It also identifies where arbitrary depen-
dence, unknown directional calibration, selected loadings and underestimated bounds fail. The independent-scale

I. Revised mathematical contribution
For a normalized flat orthogonal contrast in the full Gaussian tensor mean experiment, fixed order K￿3, positive
fixed Tucker or TT caps, known tau and supplied M, write ell=log(ep) and x=M/(tau sqrt(ell)). Honest expected
length at zero and worst case both have order
𝜏√ℓ/𝑝min{𝑥, 1 + log+ 𝑥, ℓ}
(𝐾= 3),
and 𝜏√ℓ/𝑝min{𝑥, 1} for K￿4; minimax squared risk is the square of the length rate. The tensor-specific content
is the full-data residual and harmonic-mixture argument.
Special growing-cap/loading results and adaptation
boundaries broaden this claim within explicit limits. R1 repairs the estimator output of an executable interval
while asserting only its proved noise-scale risk.
