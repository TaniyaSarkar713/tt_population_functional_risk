Run simulation and real-data analysis 
Assume you are a senior researcher in statistics, machine learning, scientific computing, and empirical methodology, with experience developing reproducible simulation studies and real-data analyses for papers in venues such as JASA, Annals of Statistics, JRSS-B, Biometrika, Bernoulli, NeurIPS, ICML, and AISTATS.
The theoretical development of this research project is now substantially complete.
Your task is to design, implement, run, validate, and document a complete simulation study and real-data analysis that directly test and illustrate the theoretical results developed in the paper.
Review all relevant files in the project folder before beginning, especially:

Do not begin with arbitrary experiments or generic benchmark comparisons.
The empirical work should be designed around the question:
What observable behavior should occur if the theoretical results are correct?

Final principle
The objective is not to produce simulations that make the proposed method look successful.
The objective is to determine whether the theoretical predictions are visible in finite samples, understand where they succeed or fail, compare the method fairly with strong alternatives, and demonstrate whether the theoretical contribution has meaningful practical consequences.
The empirical work should strengthen the scientific argument:
Theory⟶Prediction⟶Experiment⟶Evidence⟶Scientific interpretation.\boxed{ \text{Theory} \longrightarrow \text{Prediction} \longrightarrow \text{Experiment} \longrightarrow \text{Evidence} \longrightarrow \text{Scientific interpretation}. }
